Good to know

Is the sauna really private?

Yes. Your session is for your group only.

What should I bring?

Bring water and something comfortable to wear. Call us if you have questions before your first visit.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

We ask for at least 12 hours notice. Call us or use your booking confirmation to change your appointment.

Can I book for a group?

Dry sauna fits up to 4 people. Book online or call us for larger groups.
