Good to know

Is the sauna really private?

Yes. Your session is for your group only.

What should I bring?

Bring water and something comfortable to wear. Call us if you have questions before your first visit.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

We ask for at least 12 hours notice. Call us or use your booking confirmation to change your appointment.

Can I book for a group?

Dry sauna fits up to 4 people. Book online or call us for larger groups.

Does Fire and Ice Spa still offer cryotherapy?

No. We no longer offer cryotherapy. We are a private sauna spa with traditional Russian, dry and infrared sauna rooms.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Booking ahead is encouraged, especially on weekends, but walk-ins are welcome when a room is free. Weekdays usually have the most openings.

Where are you located?

115 Eastern Ave (Papa Gino's Plaza), Dedham, MA 02026. About 20 minutes from downtown Boston, close to Westwood, Norwood, Needham and West Roxbury.

Are there any sauna deals right now?

Yes. All saunas are 10% off every Wednesday from November 11, 2026 through March 25, 2027. Mention the deal when you book.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards and packages are available in our online store at store.fireicespa.com.
